Understanding the APGAR Score

Designed by Dr. Virginia Apgar in 1952, the APGAR score is a rapid method for assessing the clinical status of the newborn infant at 1 minute and 5 minutes after birth. It provides a standardized mechanism to record fetal-to-neonatal transition.

Clinical Interpretations

  • 7-10: Normal. The infant is transitioning well. Routine post-delivery care is appropriate.
  • 4-6: Moderately depressed. The infant requires interventions such as stimulation and supplemental oxygen.
  • 0-3: Severely depressed. Immediate, full neonatal resuscitation protocols must be initiated.
