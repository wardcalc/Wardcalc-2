Understanding the NIHSS

The National Institutes of Health Stroke Scale (NIHSS) is a systematic, quantitative assessment tool used to measure stroke-related neurologic deficit. It is fundamentally integrated into stroke pathways globally.

The score provides a baseline to evaluate patient improvement or deterioration and is a crucial determinant for eligibility for reperfusion therapies, such as intravenous Alteplase (tPA) or endovascular mechanical thrombectomy.
