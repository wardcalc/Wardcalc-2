Understanding the NIHSS

The National Institutes of Health Stroke Scale (NIHSS) is a systematic assessment tool that provides a quantitative measure of stroke-related neurologic deficit. It evaluates 11 specific domains, including level of consciousness, language, neglect, visual-field loss, extraocular movement, motor strength, ataxia, dysarthria, and sensory loss.

Clinical Interpretations

  • 0: No stroke symptoms.
  • 1-4: Minor stroke.
  • 5-15: Moderate stroke.
  • 16-20: Moderate to severe stroke.
  • 21-42: Severe stroke.
