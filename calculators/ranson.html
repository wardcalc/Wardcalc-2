Understanding Ranson's Criteria

Introduced in 1974, Ranson's criteria comprise 11 clinical signs to objectively evaluate the severity of acute pancreatitis. Five factors are assessed at admission to help establish an early prognosis, and an additional six are assessed 48 hours later to capture the systemic inflammatory response and third-spacing of fluids.
