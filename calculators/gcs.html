Understanding the GCS

The Glasgow Coma Scale provide a standardized method to assess impairment of conscious level. It is used globally in emergency departments and ICUs, especially for patients with head injuries.

Clinical Threshold: A score of 8 or less is generally considered the threshold where a patient can no longer protect their own airway and requires intubation.
