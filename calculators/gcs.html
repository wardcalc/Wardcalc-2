Understanding the GCS

The Glasgow Coma Scale provides a practical, standardized method to assess impairment of conscious level in response to defined stimuli. It is essential in traumatic brain injury protocols.

Clinical Interpretations

  • 13-15: Mild impairment. Usually indicates a normal state or mild traumatic brain injury (TBI).
  • 9-12: Moderate brain injury. Requires urgent imaging and close neurological monitoring.
  • 3-8: Severe brain injury. A score of 8 or less is the universally accepted threshold for securing a definitive airway via intubation.
