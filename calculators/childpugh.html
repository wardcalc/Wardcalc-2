Understanding the Child-Pugh Score

The Child-Pugh score is a globally recognized system for assessing the prognosis of chronic liver disease, primarily cirrhosis. It provides a reliable estimate of disease severity and survival, which is crucial for surgical risk assessment and medication dosing.

  • Class A (5-6 points): Well-compensated disease. Good prognosis.
  • Class B (7-9 points): Significant functional compromise. Hepatology referral indicated.
  • Class C (10-15 points): Decompensated disease. Poor prognosis, urgent transplant assessment required.
