Understanding eGFR and CKD-EPI

The estimated Glomerular Filtration Rate (eGFR) is the best overall index of kidney function. The 2021 CKD-EPI Creatinine equation is the gold standard recommended by the National Kidney Foundation, as it provides a highly accurate estimate without requiring a race coefficient.

It is vital to check the eGFR before prescribing renally excreted drugs (e.g., Metformin, DOACs, antibiotics) to prevent accidental toxicity.
