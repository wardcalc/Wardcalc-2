Understanding the Centor Score

The Centor Score helps clinicians determine the likelihood of Group A Streptococcal (GAS) pharyngitis. By estimating this probability, it guides the judicious use of rapid antigen detection testing (RADT) and empiric antibiotics, reducing unnecessary prescriptions and antimicrobial resistance.
