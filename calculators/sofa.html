Understanding the SOFA Score

The Sequential Organ Failure Assessment (SOFA) score tracks a patient's status during an ICU stay to determine the extent of a person's organ function or rate of failure. Crucially, the Third International Consensus Definitions for Sepsis and Septic Shock (Sepsis-3) defined sepsis as a life-threatening organ dysfunction caused by a dysregulated host response to infection, quantified as an acute change in total SOFA score ≥ 2 points.
