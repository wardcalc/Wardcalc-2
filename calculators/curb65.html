Understanding CURB-65

CURB-65 is a widely recommended severity score for Community-Acquired Pneumonia (CAP) adopted by the British Thoracic Society. It utilizes easily obtainable clinical variables to assist physicians in deciding whether a patient can be safely treated as an outpatient, requires ward admission, or needs intensive care.

Clinical Interpretations & Triage

  • Score 0-1: Low mortality risk. Suitable for outpatient management with oral antibiotics.
  • Score 2: Moderate mortality risk. Consider short-stay hospital admission or intensive hospital-at-home monitoring.
  • Score 3-5: High mortality risk. Mandates hospital admission. A score of 4 or 5 should trigger critical care (ICU/HDU) assessment.

References: Lim WS, van der Eerden MM, Laing R, et al. Defining community acquired pneumonia severity on presentation to hospital: an international derivation and validation study. Thorax. 2003;58(5):377-382.
