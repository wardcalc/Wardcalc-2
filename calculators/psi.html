Understanding the Pneumonia Severity Index (PSI)

The Pneumonia Severity Index, also known as the PORT score, is a comprehensive clinical prediction rule designed to calculate the probability of morbidity and mortality among patients with community-acquired pneumonia (CAP). While more complex than CURB-65, it is considered more robust due to the inclusion of age, specific comorbidities, and vital signs.
