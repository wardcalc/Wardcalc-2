Understanding the MEWS Score

The Modified Early Warning Score (MEWS) is a simple, physiological scoring system used in hospitals worldwide to quickly identify patients at risk of catastrophic clinical deterioration.

By regularly measuring vital signs, nurses and junior doctors can calculate a score that acts as a definitive "trigger" for escalating care. A score of 5 or more is statistically associated with an increased likelihood of ICU admission or cardiac arrest.
